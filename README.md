# My Favorite Movie: Coco

My favorite movie is **Coco**, an animated movie by Disney and Pixar. It tells the story of Miguel, a young boy who dreams of becoming a musician.

## Main Characters

- **Miguel** - A boy who loves music.
- **Héctor** - A man who helps Miguel.
- **Mamá Imelda** - Miguel's great-great-grandmother.

## Why I Like This Movie

I like *Coco* because it teaches the importance of family, love, and following our dreams.
